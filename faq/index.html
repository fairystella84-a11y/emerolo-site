Frequently asked questions

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Most answers are here. Emerolo has no accounts and we never see your data, so we cannot look at or fix anything inside your own app.

Your data

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. There is no sign-up, no account password and no server of ours.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, encrypted. It never reaches us. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I keep a backup?

Go to ⚙ › Device › Backups. The backup goes to your own Google Drive, locked with a password only you know. Neither Google nor we can read it.

I changed phone. How do I move my data?

On the old phone tap «Back up now». On the new one, open Emerolo, connect the same Google Drive and enter your backup password (or your recovery code).

How do I delete all my data?

In the app: ⚙ › Device › «Delete everything». For backups: on drive.google.com, ⚙ Settings › Manage apps › Emerolo › Options › Delete hidden app data. We have nothing to delete.

Codes

I forgot my PIN.

Tap «With the recovery code» and enter the code the app gave you at the start (the one you kept on paper). Then choose a new PIN.

I also lost my recovery code.

We cannot recover it: we never have it, nor any copy of it. If you have a backup on Google Drive and remember the backup password, you can uninstall the app, install it again and restore the backup.

Reminders

I don't get reminders.

1. ⚙ › Notifications: turned on. 2. Phone Settings › Apps › Emerolo › Notifications: «Allowed». 3. On some phones (e.g. Samsung) battery saving puts apps to sleep: Settings › Battery › Background usage limits, and remove Emerolo from «sleeping apps».

Subscription and payments

How much does it cost?

14 days free with every feature. Then one subscription for personal and work: €2.99 a month or €29.90 a year. Without a subscription you can see everything you wrote, but you cannot add new things.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions go only through Google Play or Apple, so you cancel there. Android: Google Play app › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Emerolo › Cancel. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Emerolo.

I want a refund.

Payments are handled by Google and Apple, and only they can refund. Google Play: play.google.com › Account › Order history › Report a problem. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. We never see payment details.

Does it issue receipts or invoices?

No. Emerolo does not issue receipts or invoices. For your subscription, Google or Apple sends the receipt.

For professionals

Where are my clients' details kept?

Only on your own device (and in the encrypted backup on your own Drive, if you turn it on). You are responsible for your clients' details. We never see them.

Does the app send messages to my clients?

No. It prepares the text and you send it from your phone (Viber, WhatsApp or SMS).

General

Which devices does it work on?

Android phones and tablets. iPhone, iPad and computer come later, with the same data through your own Google Drive.

Is it for children?

No. Emerolo is for adults (18+).

Does it have ads or tracking?

No. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Contact

I found a bug or have an idea.

Write to support@emerolo.com. We read every message, but we cannot answer each one. Please do not send personal details, screenshots with client names, or codes. This is enough: phone model, Android version and what you did before it happened.

A request about my data (GDPR) or a legal matter.

Write to privacy@emerolo.com. We answer within one month.
